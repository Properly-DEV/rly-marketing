# Company Showcase — the shape of a Properly post

The Properly company account is a showcase. Not a mood board of "website for XYZ" images with a caption, but a showcase in substantive form: every post that matters hands the reader something real — a solution, a design decision, a concrete approach to a problem they recognise. The reader follows because useful answers to their daily problems keep landing in their feed, and some of them eventually come to Properly to design the flow they can't crack themselves.

The company account delivers. A post can open with a sharp position, but it earns the follow by handing over something usable, not by leaving a thought hanging.

## The default shape (four beats)

Most substantive company posts follow this arc:

1. **Thesis** — a stated position. The problem worth solving, named plainly (Rule 3).
2. **Deepen** — make the problem real and specific, grounded in an actual project. Where it bites, what it costs, why it's hard.
3. **Solution** — what we actually designed. The payoff: the decision, the flow, the approach that answers the thesis.
4. **Visualization** — the graphic carries the solution. Post and image are one unit: the image shows the thing the post describes, not a decorative screenshot.

Not every post is a full case (a testimonial or a reaction stays short). But the substantive ones deliver, in this order.

## Ground it in the work — and mind the naming

Map the topic to a real project and show how we did it: the problem we hit, our solution. Naming follows §5.

- **KYC / compliance / trading / payments → NDAX.** Nameable (§5). Show how we designed the verification step, the Pro Trading interface, the flow: "how we did it for NDAX, what the problem was, what we shipped."
- **RWA / security tokens / leverage UX → keep it nameless.** Describe it as "a regulated security-token platform." That project isn't on §5's named list, so show the work, never the name. Show how the user operates leverage, how the multi-asset wallet reads, how compliance shapes the flow.
- **Other topics → the §5 client that anchors that theme** (each topic pack names it). Numbers only from §5; market stats from packs stay external.

## Close on the solution, not a question

The company post ends on the result or the design decision, not on "what do you think?" or "how are you handling this?". A light engagement line is fine once the payoff has landed, but the deliverable is the solution and the reader should leave holding it.

## Stay on the channel split

On social, the "deepen" beat is about the design problem and the product, not about how the collaboration ran or how long we've been at it (guide §3: social shows the product, articles show the process). Tenure and team size are half-clauses, never the subject.

## Worked example — the compliance post, in showcase shape

Thesis → deepen → solution, with the graphic as the visualization:

> Compliance stopped sitting around the product. Now it shapes the flow.
>
> On a transfer, a verification step lands two ways. The same request reassures one user and worries another, and the difference is when it appears and whether the reason is on screen.
>
> So we timed it. In the NDAX flow the step surfaces at the moment the transfer needs it, with the reason stated and the next action clear, so it reads as the platform doing its job.
>
> [graphic: the verification step in context — the "one step, two reactions" concept]

The reader leaves with a concrete design decision (timing plus a visible reason), grounded in real work, not a question.
