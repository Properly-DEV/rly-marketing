# Properly Voice — Worked Examples

Models imitate examples more reliably than they follow rules. These are the anchors for the Properly company voice. The approved specimens are lifted from the guide's §0 (the canonical voice) and repeated here so you can hold them next to your draft while you write. The before/after pairs show the failure-to-fix motion for common cases.

## Approved specimens (the canonical voice)

These are the truth. When in doubt about how Properly sounds, match these before anything else.

**Homepage**
> H1: Design partner for the modern finance
> H2: From regulated brokers to protocols managing $15B. Eight years of financial design expertise.

**Branding service line**
> Your brand is the decision made before you speak. Before your pitch starts, before your demo, before your product, investors, partners, and clients have already formed a view. Most of the time, it's based on what they saw. We design identities that make that first impression work for you, not against you, for fintech and financial services companies.

**Product Design service line**
> Your product works. It's time it looked like it. Every drop-off, every support ticket, every "I didn't understand that step" is revenue you're leaving on the table. In financial products, a confusing interface doesn't just cost you conversion, it costs you trust. We design products that users understand without a tutorial, and that your dev team can build without guessing.

**The argument against generic AI work**
> AI generates from what already exists. It has no point of view. No ability to say "this won't work." In a world of generated average, the most valuable thing a design studio can offer is conviction. Ours is built on eight years in finance.

**The audit entry point**
> Most design studios spend the first month learning what a KYC flow is, why compliance disclaimers matter, and how your users think about money. We don't. That context is what allows us to make better decisions faster, and push back when something won't work.

**Testimonial (attributed, one paragraph)**
> "It's extremely pleasant to work with Properly, the team is very knowledgeable, adaptable, and willing to challenge the management with convincing design decisions." — Bilal Hammoud, Founder & CEO, NDAX

Notice what these share: they open on the reader's situation, they carry a real position, they use plain contractions, sentence length swings, and every claim is either a concrete fact or something only Properly could say.

## Before / after

**A LinkedIn post about a client result**

Before (generic, AI-flavoured):
> We're thrilled to share that we've partnered with Spark, one of the most innovative lending protocols in DeFi! 🚀 Through our collaborative approach and passion for seamless design, we helped them build a world-class product experience. It's not just about aesthetics, it's about trust. We couldn't be more proud of what we achieved together!

Everything wrong here: "thrilled," "innovative," "collaborative approach," "passion," "seamless," "world-class" (all banned), the "it's not just about X, it's about Y" negative comparison, the rocket emoji mid-post, the motivational close, and zero real specifics.

After (Properly voice, numbers from §5):
> Spark started with $0 and a blank page. Three years later, the protocol holds $9.7B in TVL, built by the same team the whole way through.
>
> Lending protocols don't fail because the smart contracts break. They fail because a first-time lender can't tell what risk they're taking. So that's where we spent the work: making the risk legible before anyone commits a dollar.
>
> Same team, three years, from zero. That's the part you can't fake.

**A website body block (Design Partner line)**

Before:
> Our embedded design team leverages deep industry expertise to empower your organization with a holistic, end-to-end design solution that scales seamlessly with your evolving needs.

Wrong: "leverages," "empower," "holistic," "solution," "seamlessly," passive corporate mush, says nothing a competitor couldn't paste.

After:
> Design needs don't arrive in neat project shapes. One month it's a product feature. Next month it's a pitch deck that has to ship in a week. A Design Partner is a dedicated senior team that covers all of it, embedded in how you work, learning your context once and never starting over.
