# Fraud Prevention as Product Experience — topic & keyword pack

## What it is
Fraud and scam protection is moving out of the back office and into the product: detecting suspicious payments, communicating risk, blocks, alerts, safe contact, recovery flows, and user education. It's increasingly co-owned by Product, Engineering, Compliance, and Operations, with dedicated product roles built around fraud. The next generation of fintech products designs financial-crime prevention as a core product capability, not a hidden compliance function.

## Why it's rising (market context — external, dated snapshot; NOT Properly proof points)
- Revolut is a live example: it now runs dedicated fraud-focused product and risk roles (including a Head of Product for Fraud) building AI-centric fraud systems and more intuitive interactions that support trust and compliance. It communicates protection to users directly (trust, customer protection, act fast, suspicious activity, safe decisions), not buried in a help center.
- External example only. Revolut is not a Properly client.
