# Agentic Finance — topic & keyword pack

## What it is
AI agents are starting to not just advise but act: finding offers, managing subscriptions, initiating payments, optimising spend, handling treasury, or making investment decisions within set rules. The hard part won't be whether an agent can pay. It's designing who grants authority, what the limits are, which decisions it makes alone, when it must ask, what's reversible, and who's liable for a mistake.

## Why it's rising (market context — external, dated snapshot; NOT Properly proof points)
- Agentic commerce and agent-to-agent infrastructure are moving past experiments. Google is developing an Agent2Agent protocol, Anthropic MCP, Microsoft agent-control systems, and Mastercard and Visa are building payment rails that give agents identity, permission scope, and safe execution.
