# ETF Adoption & Productization — topic & keyword pack

## What it is
ETFs are becoming one of the main ways retail investors build long-term positions and get exposure to AI, crypto, global markets, and income strategies. The interesting shift for fintech isn't the ability to buy an ETF. It's the growing need to design products that help people choose, understand, and stick with a strategy.

## Why it's rising (market context — external, dated snapshot; NOT Properly proof points)
- Global ETF assets reached ~$21.91T in April 2026, with YTD inflows of ~$856.38B, above prior records.

## Narrative angles for Properly (ready-to-use theses)
- **"ETFs are turning investment strategies into products."** Broadest, most strategic. Positions Properly as a partner for wealth-tech and asset management.
- **"In investing, distribution is becoming a product design problem."** The most buyable for firms. Speaks straight to their problem: standing out in a crowded category.
- **"Familiar wrappers can make unfamiliar risk feel safe."** Sharpest thought-leadership. For fintechs signaling compliance maturity and responsible UX.
- **"As investing gets more accessible, the product challenge isn't more choices. It's helping people make better ones."** Most user-centred and universal. Reaches all of wealth-tech: brokers, robo-advisory, crypto, retirement, wealth apps.
