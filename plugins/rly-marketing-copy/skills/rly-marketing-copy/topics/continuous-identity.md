# Continuous Identity & Adaptive Trust — topic & keyword pack

## What it is
Identity verification is moving from single checkpoints (password, SMS, KYC, a document) toward continuous risk assessment. Systems read signals like device, location, interaction rhythm, and behavioral biometrics to keep judging whether the account's real owner is still present. The shift is from "are you authorised now?" to "how sure are we it's still you?".

## Why it's rising (market context — external, dated snapshot; NOT Properly proof points)
- Fraud, account takeover, and deepfake impersonation are rising, alongside pressure for faster, less frustrating login. Firms want fewer visible verification steps but more security, via adaptive authentication and continuous risk monitoring. The same data used to verify can also feed systems that generate fake identities and deepfakes.
