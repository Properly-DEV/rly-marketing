# Open Finance & Financial Aggregation — topic & keyword pack

## What it is
Financial products are moving from single services (investing, an account, savings) toward unified wealth platforms. The hard part isn't pulling every account into one dashboard. It's turning scattered financial data into an experience that helps someone actually understand their situation, make decisions, and reach goals.

## Why it's rising (market context — external, dated snapshot; NOT Properly proof points)
- Sharesies is a live example of the direction: 1M+ customers across NZ and Australia and $12B+ entrusted to the platform, combining investing, ETFs, retirement, saving, spending, crypto, and insurance.
- The financial aggregation market is projected to grow from ~$8.0B (2026) to ~$21.9B (2033), driven by open banking and API-based data sharing.
- Treat all of the above as external, dated context. Verify before quoting; never present as a Properly outcome.

## Narrative angles for Properly (ready-to-use theses)
- **"From investing app to unified wealth platform."** Best top-level angle. Fintechs start with one function and grow into where users manage most of their financial life; Properly understands products that outgrow a single feature.
- **"Financial products are converging. User journeys need to converge too."** Strongest design-partner angle. Leads naturally to information architecture, cross-product journeys, and design systems while scaling.
- **"A single financial view is not the same as financial clarity."** The most Properly UX angle. Aggregation alone gives no value; the product has to show what matters, what needs action, and how decisions connect.
- **"Open finance turns data access into a product design problem."** Most mature, industry angle. Value appears only when consent, privacy, data quality, connection errors, and the real moment-of-benefit are well designed.
- **"The next financial dashboard shouldn't show everything. It should help people see what matters."** Great post hook for data-heavy products: prioritize, understand, act.
